Delete Account and Data

You can delete your WatPhasa account in the app under Account > Delete account. You can also request deletion by email without installing or signing in to the app.

Request Deletion by Email

Send your request to contact@wpprecisionsystems.co.uk from the email address linked to your WatPhasa account. Ask us to delete your account and associated app data. Do not include your password.

What Will Be Deleted

When the request is completed, we delete your account and associated app data, including your profile, favorites, practice progress and scores, and any subscription status stored in our app database.

What May Be Retained

We may retain limited records for legal, security, fraud prevention, or payment compliance. Google Play may retain purchase records under its own policies.

Google Play Subscriptions

Deleting your WatPhasa account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. To stop future charges, manage it in Google Play subscriptions.
