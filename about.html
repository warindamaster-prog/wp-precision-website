About WP Precision Systems

An engineer-led partner for calibration, compliance and test

WP Precision Systems Ltd is a Nottingham-based engineering services and software company supporting manufacturers across the Midlands. We help ISO 9001 manufacturers keep their test systems, measuring equipment and quality records under control, without adding headcount.

Member of East Midlands Chamber.

Why we exist

In many small and medium-sized manufacturers, calibration and test equipment are managed on spreadsheets by people who already have a full-time job. It usually works, until an audit or a customer query shows the gaps: an overdue instrument, a missing certificate, or a test system nobody has checked in years.

We started WP Precision Systems to give local manufacturers a practical partner who understands both the paperwork and the production floor.

How we work

  1. Clarify the requirement. We start with your audit dates, equipment and the problem you want solved.
  2. Organise the information. We bring registers, certificates and records into one clear picture.
  3. Improve the control. We fix the gaps with practical actions that suit your team.
  4. Hand it over. You get a system your own people can run and stand behind in an audit.

Our working principles

  • Build control before problems become risks
  • Keep evidence clear, traceable and audit-ready
  • Make systems practical for the people who use them
  • Improve quality, test and process performance

Where we work

Based in Nottingham, we work on site across the East Midlands, including Nottingham, Derby and Leicester, and remotely across the UK. We also support UK manufacturers with operations in Thailand.
